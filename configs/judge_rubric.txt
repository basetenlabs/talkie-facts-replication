against the question, reference_answer, reference_fact, and any hypothetical rules

Return one label:
CORRECT: Fully answers what was asked, with all required details correct, and no
incorrect factual statements or incorrect arithmetic in the entire response.
PARTIALLY_CORRECT: Answers most of the key requested information correctly but
omits some required details. Contains no incorrect statements. This is incomplete

extra factual assertion whose truth you cannot reliably determine. Explain the
uncertainty. Do not silently assume an unsupported extra assertion is true or false.

Paraphrases, aliases, translations, number words, equivalent date formats and
unambiguous unit conversions are allowed. A brief answer inherits the event and

is not itself incorrect. Assess all text actually present; never invent a continuation.

contains no wrong/uncertain claim. A quoted wrong answer explicitly rejected as
wrong is not an asserted false claim. An explicit correction retracts the earlier
claim; conflicting answers without resolution fail. Do not reward verbosity.

Strict accuracy counts CORRECT only. Lenient accuracy counts CORRECT plus
PARTIALLY_CORRECT. REVIEW remains separate in the denominator for both lower bounds.
